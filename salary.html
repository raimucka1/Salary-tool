How pay is worked out

Basic covers 9.25 h for each contracted day worked.
Overtime 1.5 on hours above 46.25 in a Sun–Sat week. Holiday, bereavement, jury service, maternity and paternity hours count toward that 46.25.
Night premium 22:00–06:00, Monday to Friday. Weekend premium all hours Saturday and Sunday. Where the two overlap you get the higher one, never both.
Pension is salary sacrifice, taken off before tax. Total Pay is the figure after it comes off, and tax and NI are both calculated on that.
Holiday is paid at the higher of contractual pay or your 52-week average, so the day rate is personal to you and moves each period. Set it in Settings from your latest slip.

Set these yourself

Rates and rules are already filled in. Four things differ per person and start blank: tax code, pension percentage, USDAW subs and the holiday day rate. All under Settings.
PAYE is an estimate. Real PAYE is cumulative across the tax year; this works non-cumulatively, so expect a difference of well under a pound. National Insurance is exact.
Bank holiday premium is not built yet. The contract gives 50% on eight bank holidays; the app does not apply it.
Everything stays on your own phone in browser storage. Nobody else can see it. Export a backup under Settings before clearing browsing data or changing phone.
